# Stage 05b — multi-currency — SPEC

Slice: **multi-currency**, building on the accepted `ledger-core` slice. Balances become **per currency code** (`'USD'`, `'EUR'`, …) instead of one implicit balance, and D1 (balance never negative) must hold independently for every currency, not just in aggregate.

Full document committed to `docs/cascade/05b-multi-currency.md` (commit `acbc8a4`, "spec: 05b multi-currency"). Summary:

## Stories (PRD FR-3)
- **US-1**: credit/debit/capture/refund take a currency code, tracked separately per currency.
- **US-2**: no-currency calls still work and hit the implicit `'USD'` bucket — `ledger-core` callers don't break.
- **US-3**: a shortfall in one currency is never covered by another's balance.

Given/When/Then acceptance criteria AC-1…AC-5 are in the committed doc, including the key new invariant (AC-5): overdrawing currency X must reject even when another currency is flush, and must leave every currency's balance unchanged by the rejected amount.

## Design sketch
`Ledger.balances: dict[str, int]` replaces the single `balance` int. `credit`/`debit`/`capture` gain an optional `currency=DEFAULT_CURRENCY` ("USD") param. `captures[cid]` stores `(amount, currency)` so `refund(cid, amount)` replays in the captured currency without the caller repeating it — this also structurally prevents a refund from paying out in the wrong currency. A `balance` property (`balances.get("USD", 0)`) preserves the plain-attribute read the existing law/AC tests use.
